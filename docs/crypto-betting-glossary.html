Crypto Betting Glossary: 40+ Terms Every Bettor Should Know

By Leo · 2026-05-10

Crypto betting blends blockchain jargon with sports and casino terminology. This glossary covers 50+ terms you'll encounter across crypto sportsbooks and casinos.

A

Accumulator — A multi-leg bet where every selection must win to pay out. Higher payout, exponentially lower probability. For accumulator strategies in Brazil, see BTC Betting Guide.

AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) — Structuring content so AI search engines can extract and cite it directly in generated answers.

Altcoin — Any cryptocurrency other than Bitcoin. Common at sportsbooks: ETH, LTC, DOGE, TRX.

B

Bankroll — Money set aside exclusively for betting. Standard management: risk 1-5% per wager.

Bitcoin (BTC) — The most widely accepted cryptocurrency at online sportsbooks. Balances often displayed in mBTC (0.001 BTC).

Blockchain — Distributed, immutable ledger recording every crypto transaction. Enables provably fair verification and transparent deposits.

Bonus Wagering Requirement — How many times you must bet through a bonus before withdrawing. 40x on $100 = $4,000 in qualifying bets.

C

Cashout — Settling a bet before the event ends to lock in profit or cut losses. The price always includes the book's margin.

Cold Wallet — An offline crypto wallet (Ledger, Trezor). Best for long-term holdings, not daily betting.

Crash Game — A multiplier climbs from 1.00x until it randomly crashes; cash out in time or lose your stake. Usually provably fair.

Cryptocurrency — Digital currency secured by cryptography on a blockchain, enabling pseudonymous betting and global access.

D

dApp — A decentralized application on a blockchain. Some betting platforms run as dApps with no single entity controlling funds.

Dead Heat — When selections tie, your payout is divided by the number of tied participants.

Decimal Odds — Standard format on crypto sportsbooks. Odds of 2.50 on a $1 bet return $2.50 total. Stake x odds = payout.

DeFi — Decentralized finance on blockchain. Some DeFi protocols host prediction markets and betting pools via smart contracts.

Deposit Bonus — A percentage match on your deposit (e.g., "100% up to 1 BTC"). Always check the wagering requirement.

E

Edge — The statistical advantage one side holds. Sharp bettors seek edges against the sportsbook's lines.

EV (Expected Value) — Average profit or loss per bet over time. +EV = profitable long-term. Formula: (win probability x profit) - (loss probability x stake).

F

Fiat — Government-issued currency (USD, EUR, BRL). "Fiat on-ramp" means converting fiat to crypto for deposits.

Fractional Odds — Odds as a fraction (5/1 = $5 profit per $1 staked). UK standard, rarely seen on crypto platforms.

G

Gas Fee — Fee paid to Ethereum validators. Fluctuates with congestion and can make small ETH deposits uneconomical.

GEO — Geographic targeting of content, offers, or platform availability by region to comply with local regulations.

H

Handicap — A market giving one side a virtual advantage. Asian handicap uses half-goal increments to eliminate draws.

Hash — A fixed-length cryptographic fingerprint of data. Provably fair systems publish the result hash before each round.

HMAC — Hash-based message authentication code. Used in provably fair systems to chain game results across rounds.

Hot Wallet — An internet-connected wallet for active transactions. Convenient but more exposed to hacks than cold storage.

House Edge — The casino's mathematical advantage per game, as a percentage. 4% house edge = 96% RTP.

I

In-Play Betting — Betting after an event starts with live-updating odds. Crypto deposits settle fast enough to fund mid-game wagers. For cricket markets in India, see BitcoinBetPro.

K

KYC (Know Your Customer) — Identity verification required by regulated platforms. Many crypto sportsbooks skip KYC for crypto-only users.

L

Limit — Maximum bet a sportsbook accepts on a market. Consistent winners get "limited" — their max reduced.

Liquidity — Money available for matching on exchanges. Low liquidity means large bets move the line against you.

M

Margin — The sportsbook's built-in profit. A fair coin flip is 2.00/2.00; books offer 1.91/1.91 (~4.5% margin).

Martingale — Doubling your bet after every loss to recover. Fails given finite bankrolls and table limits.

Mempool — Queue of unconfirmed blockchain transactions. Congestion = slower deposits, higher fees.

Miner Fee — Fee incentivizing miners/validators to process your transaction. Higher fee = faster confirmation.

Moneyline — A straight win bet with no spread. Crypto platforms show decimal equivalents of American odds.

N

Nonce — A number used once in provably fair algorithms. Combines with the server seed to generate outcomes, preventing manipulation.

O

Odds — The ratio defining potential payout relative to stake. Decimal 4.00 implies 25% probability. For format conversions, try the odds converter at CoinBetPro.

Over/Under — A bet on whether a stat (goals, points) finishes above or below a line. Over 2.5 goals wins if 3+ are scored.

P

P2P (Peer-to-Peer) — Bettors wager against each other, not a house. Exchanges and prediction markets use this model.

Parlay — North American term for accumulator. All legs must win.

Payout — Total returned on a winning bet, including stake. Payout = stake x decimal odds.

Provably Fair — A cryptographic system letting players verify game outcomes weren't manipulated. Server commits a hashed seed pre-round; revealed afterward for verification. Audit with a provably fair verifier.

R

RNG (Random Number Generator) — Algorithm determining casino game outcomes. Provably fair systems let you verify output directly.

ROI — Net profit as a percentage of total wagered. 5% ROI = $5 profit per $100 staked.

RTP (Return to Player) — Percentage of wagered money a game returns over its lifetime. Always check before choosing a slot.

S

Satoshi (sat) — Smallest Bitcoin unit: 0.00000001 BTC. Many sportsbooks display balances in sats.

Seed (Client/Server) — Inputs to a provably fair algorithm. Server seed is hashed pre-round; client seed is player-chosen. Together they produce the outcome.

SEO — Search engine optimization — how crypto betting sites drive organic traffic to reviews and guides.

Spread — Point margin handicapping a favorite. The team must win by more than the spread to cover.

Stablecoin — Crypto pegged to fiat (usually USD). USDT and USDC let you bet without BTC price volatility.

Stake (unit) — The amount wagered on a single bet. Not to be confused with Stake.com, the platform.

T

TRC-20 — Token standard on Tron. TRC-20 USDT dominates crypto betting deposits: sub-$1 fees, ~3-second confirmations.

U

USDT (Tether) — Most used stablecoin, pegged 1:1 to USD. Available on TRC-20, ERC-20, BEP-20; TRC-20 dominates betting deposits.

V

Variance — How much short-term results deviate from EV. Slots and parlays: high variance. Blackjack and spreads: low.

VIG (Vigorish) — The sportsbook's commission on bets, also called "juice." Standard: ~4.5-5%.

W

Wallet — Software or hardware storing your crypto private keys for depositing and withdrawing.

Whale — A bettor placing extremely large wagers, sometimes moving lines on smaller books.

Z

Zero-Confirmation — Crediting a crypto deposit before it's confirmed on-chain. Some sportsbooks accept the double-spend risk for faster UX.
